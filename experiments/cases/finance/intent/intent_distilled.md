# Mission
Establish a pipeline for processing quarterly financial data into a comprehensive, accurate written report for stakeholders, ensuring regulatory compliance and data integrity. The report must clearly articulate revenue and expense insights and meet professional and compliance standards.

# Distilled goals
1. Retrieve accurate and up-to-date raw revenue and expense data in a timely manner.
2. Accurately classify revenue as either "high revenue" (above $50,000) or "standard revenue" (up to $50,000) and provide analysis with at least 10 characters of meaningful detail.
3. Ensure all high-revenue entries undergo a tax audit by a Tax Specialist, with substantive and non-empty findings.
4. Require all revenue classifications and audit findings to be explicitly verified by a Tax Verifier prior to inclusion in the final report.
5. Analyze operating expenses with detailed descriptions (over 10 characters) that highlight trends and support insights for the report.
6. Compile a cohesive, clear, and professional written quarterly financial report based on verified and approved analyses.
7. Deliver the final report to the designated recipient (Fetcher) for stakeholder review, archiving, or external submission.

# Constraints and policies
- Revenue exceeding $50,000 must be flagged for tax audits ("high revenue" classification).
- Revenue below or equal to $50,000 must be classified as "standard revenue."
- All revenue and expense analyses must exceed 10 characters of meaningful detail.
- Tax audits for high-revenue entries must be substantive and must not be empty.
- The Tax Verifier must explicitly approve all classifications and audits before inclusion in the final report.
- Every team member must adhere to deadlines and maintain clear communication to prevent delays or inaccuracies.
- Ethical standards in classification, auditing, and data handling must be upheld.

# Completion signal
The team is finished when a finalized, accurate quarterly finance report, meeting all outlined goals and compliance criteria, has been delivered to the Fetcher.
